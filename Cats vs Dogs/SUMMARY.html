Key Takeaways

  • Peak Performance: The model reached its optimal balance of training and validation loss at Epoch 13 (Val Loss: 0.4169, Val Accuracy: 86.12%).
  • Overfitting Observed: Beyond Epoch 13, the training accuracy continued to climb up to ~96% while the validation loss diverged and became unstable, triggering Early Stopping.
  • Recommendation: Adding data augmentation (e.g., rotation, flipping, zooming) and stronger regularization or pre-trained architectures (like ResNet or MobileNet) is recommended for better generalization on small datasets (~800 samples).
